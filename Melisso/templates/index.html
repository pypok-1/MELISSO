PURE CRAFTSMANSHIP

Where the sea meets stone and wind

In the unique microclimate of southern Crete, rains are absent for almost 300 days a year. To survive under the unrelenting sun, wild thyme shrubs secrete ultra-concentrated nectar packed with unprecedented levels of essential oils and antioxidants.

no_drinks

No sugar feeding or thermal processing

We never feed our bees with sugar syrup and never heat honey above natural hive temperature (+35°C).

all_inclusive

Ethical bee-centric beekeeping

We leave honeybee families over 40% of their own honey for wintering. Zero chemical treatments or antibiotics.

pin

Numbered hand-poured jars

Each jar is sealed with beeswax and signed by hand with exact GPS coordinates of the harvest slope.
